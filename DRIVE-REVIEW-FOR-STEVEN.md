## Where things stand

## 1. The structure is complete

**All 18 master folders** now break down the same way. Each one opens straight
into its categories — for example `07 FINANCE, BANKING & TAX - Banking &
Statements`, `06 LEGAL - Disputes & Claims`.

Getting there involved removing a duplicated layer. Fourteen of the master
folders contained an older numbered folder left over from an earlier attempt,
so every path ran through two numbered folders to reach anything. Those 87
subfolders were lifted up one level and the empty shells removed.

**All 15 properties** now carry the same 14 categories, named after the
property — `512 West Edison - Deeds & Ownership`, and so on. **214 folders in
total.**

Legal matters, specific deals and dated statement batches kept their own names
rather than being renamed, so `Kollar v Stewart — Matter Records` is still
findable by that name.

## 3. What reading the documents turned up

Files are being sorted by reading them, not by their names. That has caught a
number of things a name-based sort would have got wrong:

**Documents filed under the wrong idea of what they were.** `1129.pdf` and
`24255.pdf` are the 2022 trust agreements. `1919 Marquette Nailon Williams.pdf`
is an executed lease. `PARKMOR PLAZA Warranty.pdf` is a 20-year Carlisle
roofing warranty, not a deed. A file named only
`c1656679-b3aa-40fa-851b-21f2bbdd8164.pdf` is the CBRE Offering Memorandum for
Mini Mountain RV Resort.

**Three 2012 leases that looked current.** Three files titled `new creation
24255 huron ... lease` read, in the text, "made and entered into this 26th day
of May 2012". On their names alone they would have been filed as live leases
for a property you still own.

**Files that are not records at all.** A number of items at the top of the
Drive are saved web pages — a OneDrive photo screen, a ChatGPT window, an
airline booking page — captured as documents. They have been left alone.

**Archives that were already extracted.** Six folders named `Vault - ...
(Unzipped Files)` are sitting at the top of the Drive. The Mini Mountain,
Chiafos and Value Add Retail archives had already been unpacked once. They have
been gathered into the right properties so nobody unpacks them again.

## 4. Decisions needed before the next stage

**a. Two folders, one building.** `Park More Plaza` and `Value Add Retail -
2024 Roof` are the same asset — 1104 West Bristol Street, Elkhart. Four
documents describe it under three names: Parkmor Plaza, Value Add Multi-Tenant
Retail, and Shops on Bristol. Right now its records are splitting across two
folders with 28 categories between them. Nothing was merged; that is your call.

**b. Pre-2020 ownership records.** The 2020 cut-off moved two documents out of
scope that record how 512 West Edison came to be owned: a 2013 land contract
proposal and a 2016 assignment of that contract. They are parked in
`00.8 — Out of Date Scope`, not deleted. Should the date rule bend for
chain-of-title documents?

**c. Folder colours.** The green / yellow / red / purple status colours cannot
be applied through the connection being used — it can rename and move folders
but has no access to colour. Every folder's status is written into the working
record instead. Applying them is a short manual pass in Drive if you want them.

**d. Two inbox systems.** The `00 — INBOX & FILE CONTROL` folder now holds two
overlapping sets of intake folders, one numbered `00.1`–`00.9` and one not.
Three of the unnumbered ones contain live files. Merging them means comparing
documents, so nothing was touched.

## 5. What remains

Roughly **5,830 loose files** still sit at the top of the Drive. About 1,450 of
them state a date in the filename; the other 4,400 do not, and their date can
only be established by opening them — the upload date is not reliable, as the
2022 trust agreement uploaded in 2026 shows.

They are being worked through property by property, then by business area. Each
batch is logged as it completes, so the work can stop and resume without
repeating itself.
