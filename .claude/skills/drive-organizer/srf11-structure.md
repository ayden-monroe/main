## Filing rules (verbatim from the plan)

1. Keep property-specific documents inside that property's folder.
2. Use shortcuts when accounting or another department needs the same
   document. Maintain one authoritative copy.
3. Organize recurring financial records by **year → month**.
4. Name files consistently:
   `YYYY-MM-DD — Property or Company — Document Type — Description`.
5. Restrict personnel, banking, and tenant application folders to the
   appropriate people. Folder names alone do not restrict access.
6. Put unclear files and suspected duplicates into review folders before
   deciding their final location.
