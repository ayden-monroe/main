## 00 — INBOX & FILE CONTROL subfolders

Build these first; triage depends on them.

- `00.1 — New Uploads`
- `00.2 — Scanned Documents`
- `00.3 — Email Attachments`
- `00.4 — Needs Identification`
- `00.5 — Possible Duplicates`
- `00.6 — Needs Decision` (items requiring a named person's judgment)
- `00.7 — TO BE DETERMINED` (destination not established under the rules — holding only)
- `00.8 — Out of Date Scope` (dated before 2020, or no determinable date)
- `00.9 — Filing Queue & Master Index` (the tracker sheets live here)

`00.7` is a holding area, not an answer. Nothing is renamed while it sits there, and
every item in it has an open Filing Queue row with the question that would resolve it.
`00.8` exists so out-of-scope files stop being re-examined every pass; files are listed
into it for the user and only moved there with permission.

## 01 — MISSION CONTROL subfolders

`Dashboards` · `Decisions & Approvals` · `Responsibility Matrix` · `Deadlines & Follow-Up` · `Meeting Records`

## Company-function folders (04, 05, 07, 08, 10, 11, 13, 14)

These follow one pattern rather than a bespoke tree each: group by function, then by year
where records recur. Financial and tax records are organized **year → month**. Vendor and
entity folders are one folder per vendor or per entity, named for the vendor or entity.

## Where things go when it isn't obvious

- Work on a specific property stays with the property (03), not in Construction (04).
  Folder 04 holds standards, templates and crew-level material that spans properties.
- A legal matter about a property lives in 06, with a shortcut from the property folder.
  Litigation has its own discovery, evidence and deadline discipline that a property
  folder does not provide.
- A document that is both financial and property-specific lives with the property, with a
  shortcut into 07.
- Anything touching personnel, banking credentials, tenant applications, or legal evidence
  needs real Drive permissions set on the destination, not just a "Restricted" label.
