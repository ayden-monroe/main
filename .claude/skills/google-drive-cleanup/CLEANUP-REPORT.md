## F. Decisions and blockers

### 1. Folder colours cannot be applied — needs a manual pass

The Drive connector available here can change only a file's **title** and
**parent**. It has no field for folder colour. Colours were therefore **not
applied, and are not claimed to be applied**, for any folder or for any viewer.

The section 7 status for all 225 folders is recorded in section B above. To
apply them by hand: right-click each folder in Drive → Organise → colour.
Roughly 15 minutes for the 15 parents; the 210 subfolders would take longer and
may not be worth it.

### 2. A file in the Massa folder is a plaintext password list — act on this today

The file titled **`farm duane 2022 2025  properties massa`**, a Google Sheet
sitting in the *Massa and Duane farm* folder, is not a property document. It is
an unencrypted list of login credentials — usernames and passwords for email,
banking, card, alarm, and vendor accounts, some with account numbers attached.
The title does not indicate this.

It was **not moved, opened further, renamed, or copied**, per the standing
instruction to leave credentials untouched and flag them. Its contents are not
reproduced here or anywhere in this project.

Recommended, for Mr Kollar to decide: move those credentials into a password
manager and delete the sheet, or at minimum restrict the sheet's sharing. Until
then anyone with access to this Drive folder has those accounts. Nothing further
will be done with this file without instruction.

Their contents cannot be read through this connector, and the filenames do not

and no deletion candidate list is being proposed on that basis.
