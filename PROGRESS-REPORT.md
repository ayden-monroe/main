**Prepared by:** Ayden Monroe

---

## 1. Project overview

Your Google Drive had grown to several thousand files with no consistent filing
system. Documents sat loose at the top level, the same folder names appeared
several times over, and the same document often existed in four or five copies.
Finding anything meant searching and hoping.

This project puts the whole Drive into one clear filing system, so that every
document has one obvious place to live and anyone on your team can find it
without asking.

A note on what this project is *not*: there is no software being built here. The
only thing stored in the project repository is a set of working notes — a written
method, a running progress log, and a full map of every folder created. Those
notes exist so the work can be paused and picked up later without losing track of
what has already been done. All the actual work happens inside your Drive.

Two people have contributed: Ayden Monroe (5 work sessions, 14–17 September) and
an automated assistant working under supervision (4 sessions, 17 September).

### Moving to your business's filing standard — 17 September

Your business supplied its own filing plan (the "SRF 11" plan). We adopted it.

- **All 83 subfolders moved** into the numbered system your team had started,
  keeping every document inside them intact.
- **The full SRF 11 structure was then built exactly as the plan specifies** —
  14 main folders (Inbox, Company & Administration, Finance & Accounting,
  Property Portfolio, Acquisitions & Sales, Construction, Rentals, Vendors,
  Employees, Legal, Marketing, Systems, Templates, Archive) with all 86 of their
  subfolders.
- **A reusable property folder template was created** — the 12-part structure the
  plan lays out for each property (overview, acquisition, financing, permits,
  construction, financials, photos, leasing, maintenance, sale, correspondence,
  archive). Copy it, rename it to the address, and drop it under the right status.
- **76 subfolders were then re-filed** into that structure, so there is now one
  system rather than two competing ones.
- **The filing rules themselves were written up as a document inside your Drive**,
  under Systems & Operations, so the team can follow the same conventions.
